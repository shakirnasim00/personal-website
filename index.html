About

I'm a founder and builder from Vancouver who likes turning a clear problem into a product people reach for.

I studied Computer Science at Thompson Rivers University, where I also spent three seasons on the varsity basketball team and was named a CCAA Academic All-Canadian. Sport taught me most of what I know about pace, preparation, and owning outcomes.

Today I'm building Circle, a healthier alternative to social media, which I researched, designed, and shipped myself on iOS. Alongside it I lead outbound sales at Corporate Traveller, and I write on Substack about consumption, attention, and modern life.
